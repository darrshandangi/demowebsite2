Meet
Claudia

I’m Claudia Kalotay, PA-C, owner of the medical spa and a board-certified Physician Assistant with over 20 years of experience. I completed my training through the MEDEX Northwest Physician Assistant Program at the University of Washington.

As a locally owned practice, our medical spa is dedicated to providing personalized, evidence-based aesthetic care in a safe and welcoming environment. The practice is medically overseen by a local surgeon/physician to ensure the highest standards of patient care.

I specialize in facial aesthetic treatments, including injectable therapies designed to enhance your natural beauty and help eliminate wrinkles and aging. In addition to beauty enhancements, I also use injectables to relieve TMJ, muscle tension, and shoulder and neck discomfort when appropriate. I also provide weight loss education, medication and guidance to support your overall health and wellness goals.

My philosophy is simple: to help you look refreshed, feel confident, and achieve natural-looking results through honest, individualized care.
