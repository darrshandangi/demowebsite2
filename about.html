Meet the Director

Dr. Michael Ablavsky

Dr. Ablavsky is a board-certified, fellowship-trained plastic surgeon whose patients trust him for natural-looking results from face, breast, and body procedures.

His philosophy centers on listening closely to each patient's unique concerns, taking the time to answer questions with honesty, clinical precision, and genuine compassion.

Specializing in cosmetic surgery in San Antonio, he has built a practice that serves as a sanctuary for those seeking to refine their aesthetic while prioritizing safety and professional excellence.
