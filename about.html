About Me

Hi there! My name is Cassie!

I am a Certified Medical Aesthetician of 3 and 1/2 years and owner of CK Aesthetics. I started my business about 3 years ago because of the need I saw in the industry for quality results driven skincare products and services, at affordable prices.

Struggling with my own skin issues over the years is what led me to my love of skin health and skincare. I believe everyone should have access to good skincare and treatments.

I work with you as the client to help create the best skincare routine and treatment plan suited to you and your budget. My goal has always been to help you understand your skin, skincare ingredients, and help you feel beautiful and confident in your skin!
