The Founder

Our Philosophy by Claudia Kalotay PA-C

At CK Aesthetics, I believe the most beautiful results are subtle, refined, and uniquely yours. My goal is to help you look refreshed and naturally beautiful—not overdone or artificial—while allowing you to age gracefully and confidently.

With more than 20 years of experience in medicine and surgery, I bring a trained eye, clinical precision, and a personalized approach to every patient. I also believe luxury care should feel personal and relaxed—a place where you can be yourself, ask questions, and feel genuinely cared for. A place where everyone is welcome.

Whether you’re here for aesthetic treatments, facial rejuvenation, or medical weight management and wellness, my goal is simple: to help you look your best, feel your best, live your healthiest life, and still look like you.
