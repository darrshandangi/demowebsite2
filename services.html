Exfoliation & Hydro

Dermaplaning

$90

A safe and effective form of manual exfoliation to gently remove dead skin cells and vellus hair (peach fuzz) from the face. Allows for deeper product absorption, make up application, and leaves the skin with a soft, smooth, glowing complexion. Includes deep cleanse, dermaplaning, jelly mask, hot towels, and product application.

*Not recommended for rosacea skin or active acne.

Hydrodermabrasion - Hydrofacial

$95

A deep exfoliating treatment that uses vacuum suction, water, and hydro-serums to remove debris and dry/dead skin cells from the outer layer of skin, while simultaneously infusing hydration and nutrients into the skin and stimulating blood circulation and lymphatic drainage. Includes deep cleanse, enzyme mask with neck/shoulder massage, hydrodermabrasion, LED light therapy, and product application.

Perfectly safe for sensitive skin.

Microneedling

Microneedling - Face

$255

A rejuvenation treatment using the Health Canada approved E-Pen. This device uses 12 fine micro needles to puncture the skin and create a controlled injury. This triggers the bodies natural healing response to produce collagen and elastin. Great for skin texture, acne scaring, hyperpigmentation, stretch marks, fine lines and wrinkles.

*Not recommended for pregnant/nursing clients, or active acne.

Microneedling - Face + Neck

$300

Enjoy the benefits of our facial microneedling treatment, extended to include the neck area for comprehensive rejuvenation and skin tightening.

Chemical Peels

Beginner Chemical Peel - No Peel Peel

$75 - $90

A beginner chemical peel with little to no downtime or flakiness. The No Peel Peel is a mixture of acids applied to gently exfoliate the outer layers of dead skin cells and accelerate cell turnover. Great for all skin types to help with fine lines, hyperpigmentation, acne, and texture.

*Not recommended while pregnant or nursing.

Chemical Peel - Perfecting Peel

$85 - $100

Safe for all skin types and ethnicities. A mixture of acids to gently exfoliate dead skin cells and accelerate turnover. Great for improving oily skin, acne, fine lines and wrinkles, hyperpigmentation, and overall skin tone and texture. Minimal to moderate flakiness may occur.

*Not recommended while pregnant or nursing.
