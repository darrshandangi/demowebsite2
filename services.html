Medical Weight Management

What are GLP-1s?

GLP-1 stands for Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 agonists. This class of medications mimics a hormone naturally produced in the body to help control blood sugar and regulate digestion. These medications assist with weight management through several biological mechanisms:

  • Insulin Regulation: Stimulates the pancreas to release insulin when blood sugar levels are high and helps reduce the amount of sugar the liver releases.
  • Gastric Emptying: Slows the rate at which the stomach empties, helping individuals feel full more quickly and for longer.
  • Appetite Signaling: Interacts with the hypothalamus to signal satiety, reducing persistent cravings or "food noise."

Lifestyle and Nutrition

When using GLP-1 medications, maintaining a nutrient-dense diet is important because appetite is often reduced. I recommend 70-100 grams/day of protein to help preserve lean muscle mass, staying well-hydrated, and incorporating regular physical activity including resistance training.

For any specific medical advice, dosing changes, or to discuss starting a new medication, it is essential to consult directly with a licensed healthcare professional like me. I will be your go-to person at anytime and will be walking this journey with you.

Neuromodulators

Neurotoxin for Wrinkle treatment

Neurotoxins such as Botox®, Dysport®, and Jeuveau® are injectable treatments designed to temporarily relax targeted muscles that contribute to dynamic wrinkles and lines. By reducing repetitive muscle movement, neurotoxins can soften the appearance of wrinkles while helping prevent them from becoming more deeply etched over time.

At CK Aesthetics, I take a precise, individualized approach to neurotoxin treatments. Rather than simply eliminating movement, my goal is to create a refreshed, balanced appearance while preserving your natural expressions and personality.

Common treatment areas include:

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Crow’s feet
  • Bunny lines around the nose
  • Lip lines
  • Chin dimpling
  • Neck and platysmal bands
  • Jawline and masseter muscles

Treatments are customized based on your facial anatomy, muscle activity, and aesthetic goals. The goal is not to make you look “done”—but to help you look rested, refreshed, and naturally rejuvenated.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is trapezius Botox? expand_more

Trapezius Botox involves targeted injections of a neuromodulator such as Botox or Dysport into specific areas of the trapezius muscle. These treatments temporarily reduce muscle activity, which may help decrease excessive muscle tension.

How long does it last? expand_more

Many patients begin noticing effects within several days to two weeks after treatment, with benefits typically lasting several months depending on factors such as muscle activity and metabolism.
